*   **Narrative:** Global software (Odoo, Loyverse) fundamentally breaks in Africa because it assumes two things that aren't true: 100% internet reliability and simple tax laws.
*   **The Reality:** Network outages paralyze retail operations, and non-compliance with strict new Electronic Billing mandates (like Rwanda's VSDC) results in business closures.
