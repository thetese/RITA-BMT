# Virtual Data Room (VDR) Checklist

To ensure due diligence (Phase 5) goes smoothly, prepare a secure Google Drive or Dropbox folder with the following structure and populate it with the respective documents.

## 1. Corporate & Legal
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] Articles of Association / Operating Agreement
- [ ] Cap Table (Current list of owners, equity percentages, and options pool)
- [ ] List of Directors and Board Members
- [ ] IP Assignment Agreements (Ensuring the company owns all code)

## 2. Product & Technology
- [ ] `vdr_technical_architecture.md` (System Architecture & RRA Sync flows)
- [ ] API Documentation (Links to internal docs for POS/Sync)
- [ ] Proof of RRA EBM/VSDC Integration (API specs or certification letter)
- [ ] Product Roadmap (12-18 months)

## 3. Financials
- [ ] `financial_model_structure.csv` (The 3-5 Year Financial Projections)
- [ ] Historical P&L (Profit & Loss) and Cash Flow Statements (If applicable)
- [ ] Pricing Sheet (SaaS Tiers & Enterprise Licenses)

## 4. Market & Strategy
- [ ] Pitch Deck (PDF Version)
- [ ] Market Research / TAM Analysis (African B2B / ERP Market)
- [ ] Go-to-Market Strategy Document
- [ ] Competitor Analysis Matrix (Rita POS vs. Odoo vs. Loyverse vs. Local legacy)

## 5. Team
- [ ] Founder Bios & Resumes (Highlighting technical/domain expertise)
- [ ] Current Org Chart
- [ ] Hiring Plan (Post-funding)

## 6. Customers & Traction
- [ ] Active Customer List / Pilot Partners
- [ ] Signed Letters of Intent (LOIs)
- [ ] Customer Case Studies / Testimonials
- [ ] Usage Metrics (Active installations, transaction volumes)
